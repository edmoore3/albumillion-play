How to play

Seven questions, the same seven for everyone, new each day at midnight New York time. Name anything that fits. Every valid answer counts; the rarer it is, the more it is worth.

Practice rounds are seven at a time from the whole set, less the daily questions of today and the week ahead, dealt from a deck this browser keeps: nothing comes back until everything else has been played.

Type an answer and press Enter. Spelling does not have to be perfect: Enter corrects your guess to the answer it matches and locks it. Where a guess could be two answers ("Garfunkel"), the box offers the better known one; Enter again locks it, or change it. A guess that is not recognised costs nothing but the seconds it takes, so keep guessing until something lands. Short answers get 30 seconds, "artist - title" answers 45.

Each answer earns a percentage from its rank among every valid answer: the most obvious earns 1%, and an answer past the thousandth best known earns 99% however many rarer ones there are. Each question owns a seventh of the record, and your percentage decides how much of it the needle plays; at 90% and up an answer is Once In Albumillion and plays its whole seventh. Seven of them reach the label.
