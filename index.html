How to play

Seven questions, the same seven for everyone today. Name anything that fits. Every valid answer counts; the rarer it is, the more it is worth.

Practice rounds are seven at a time from the whole set, dealt from a deck this browser keeps: nothing comes back until everything else has been played.

Type freely: the matched answer shows under the box, and Enter locks it. A guess that is not recognised costs nothing but the seconds it takes, so keep guessing until something lands. Short answers get 30 seconds, "artist - title" answers 45.

Each answer earns a percentage from its rank among every valid answer: the rarest earns 100%, the most obvious 1%. Each question owns a seventh of the record, and your percentage decides how much of it the needle plays. It reaches the label only if all seven answers earn 100%.
